# Phonotactic patterns

[[Output Strictly-Local]]:
- [[Progressive Harmony]] (first vowel determines all others)
- [[Regressive Harmony]] (last vowel determines all others)

[[Weakly Deterministic]]:
- [[Dominant-Recessive Harmony]] (everything harmonizes if a specific value is present anywhere)

[[Subregular]] but not Weakly Deterministic:
- [[Sour Grapes Patterns]] (non-intervening blockers; unattested)
- [[Circumambient Unbounded]] (surrounding triggers)

Majority Rules also unattested.
