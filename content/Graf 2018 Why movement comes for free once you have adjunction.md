TL;DR:

**Motivation:** People think [[Merge]] is [[Strictly Local]] and [[Move]] is [[Tier-based Strictly Local]] because Move needs to ignore unbounded intermediate material. 

**Contribution:** But once you add [[Adjunction|adjunction]] to the grammar, and give adjuncts category features that can themselves be adjoined to, then Merge becomes TSL, and requires basically the same machinery as Move. You're just matching an X+ feature and an X- feature, and collapsing the intermediate material between them. 

**Impact:** This revitalizes Chomsky's intuition that Move and Merge are deeply similar.
